## The ideas
- Cointegration: several series each integrated of order 1 (random-walk-like), with a linear combination that is stationary. The combination is the long-run equilibrium; its value is the "disequilibrium error".
- Examples from the lecture: the term structure of rates, purchasing power parity, money demand, covered interest parity, the law of one price for equivalent assets, spot vs futures, one asset on two venues.
- Vector error correction model (VECM): dX_t = Pi X_{t-1} + lagged differences + e. The rank of Pi is the number of cointegrating relations (Johansen's approach).
- Linear state-space models: a hidden state evolves, noisy observations of it arrive. The Kalman filter updates the best estimate one observation at a time.
- Example: a time-varying CAPM, where alpha and beta are hidden states following random walks.

## Watch out
- Cointegration found in one year can break the next (a merger, a scandal, a business change). Test many pairs and about 5% will look cointegrated by luck.

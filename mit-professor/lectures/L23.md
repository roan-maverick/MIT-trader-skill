# Lecture 23: Quanto credit hedging

Instructor: Stefan Andreev (Morgan Stanley).

## The ideas
- FX basics: spot, forwards and interest-rate parity (the forward is set by the two currencies' interest rates); FX modeled as a stochastic process.
- Credit default swaps: insurance against a borrower's default, priced with a hazard rate (the instantaneous default intensity) and survival probabilities.
- Quanto CDS: the same protection paid in a different currency. For a sovereign (the euro-area crisis is the lecture's example), default and the currency's collapse are linked: when the country defaults, its currency jumps down.
- That jump makes protection in the local currency worth less than in dollars; the spread between the two quotes measures the expected devaluation on default. Pricing uses jump processes and risk-neutral expectations; hedging must handle the jump.

## In this pack
- Background only. The lesson that transfers: risks that look separate (credit, currency) can jump together exactly when it hurts.

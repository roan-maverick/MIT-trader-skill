# Lecture 11: Time series analysis II

Instructor: Peter Kempthorne. Case Study 5 (VAR models of macroeconomic series).

## The ideas
- Multivariate Wold representation: many series driven by a vector of shocks.
- Vector autoregression VAR(p): each series regressed on lags of all series. Stationary when the roots of det(I - Phi_1 z - ... - Phi_p z^p) lie outside the unit circle.
- Estimation: equation-by-equation OLS is optimal when every equation has the same regressors (shown with Kronecker products and the vec operator); maximum likelihood gives the same answer under normal errors.
- Uses: forecasting, impulse responses, Granger causality (does one series help predict another).

## In the skill
- The building block of the cointegration model in Lecture 12 and pairs.py: two prices in levels, each a random walk, inside a VAR.

## Watch out
- A VAR in many series has many parameters; with daily data it overfits fast. Keep it small.

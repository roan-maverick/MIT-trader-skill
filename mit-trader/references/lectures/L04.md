# Lecture 4: Matrix primer

Presented by the Morgan Stanley Matrix team. MIT OpenCourseWare publishes no notes or video for this session; the course page points to an outside microsite.

## What it is about
- Applied matrix tools used on a trading floor: covariance matrices, their decompositions and their use in risk and portfolio systems.

## The math to know (from Lectures 2, 10 and 15)
- A covariance matrix must be symmetric and positive semi-definite: w' S w >= 0 for every portfolio w. Estimated from limited data it can be badly conditioned, which makes inverses (and optimizers) unstable.
- Cholesky factor: S = L L' with L lower triangular; used to simulate correlated returns.
- Shrinkage (Lecture 10) and factor structure (Lecture 15) are the two standard cures for a noisy covariance matrix.

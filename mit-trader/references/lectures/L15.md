# Lecture 15: Factor modeling

Instructor: Peter Kempthorne. Case Study 7 (factor models of Treasury yields: PCA and factor analysis).

## The ideas
- Linear factor model: each asset's return = alpha + loadings x factor returns + its own noise. A few factors explain most co-movement; the covariance becomes B F B' + D, far fewer parameters.
- Three kinds: macroeconomic factors (observed series; time-series regressions), fundamental factors (company characteristics; cross-sectional regressions, as in Barra industry models), statistical factors (found from the data: factor analysis and principal components).
- Sharpe's single-index model: the market is the only factor (CAPM).
- Principal components: eigenvectors of the covariance (Lecture 2). For yields, the first three are level, slope and curvature.

## In the skill
- factors.py beta (single index), model (macro factors from ETFs: market, small minus large, tech minus market, long bonds, gold, energy) and pca (statistical factors with a noise edge).

## Watch out
- Factor loadings change over time (see the Kalman beta). Statistical factors have no fixed meaning: PC2 today may be a different story next year.

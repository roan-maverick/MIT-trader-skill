# Lecture 18: Ito calculus

Instructor: Choongbum Lee.

## The ideas
- Because (dB)^2 = dt, ordinary calculus fails for functions of Brownian motion. Ito's lemma adds the second-order term.
- The Ito integral of an adapted process (one that does not look into the future) is a martingale; Ito isometry gives its variance.
- Change of measure and Girsanov's theorem: changing the probability measure changes a Brownian motion's drift without changing its volatility. Pricing under the risk-neutral measure relies on it.

## Formulas
- Ito's lemma: df(t, X_t) = f_t dt + f_x dX + (1/2) f_xx (dX)^2.
- For GBM dS = mu S dt + sigma S dB: d(ln S) = (mu - sigma^2/2) dt + sigma dB. The log price grows at mu - sigma^2/2, not mu.

## In the skill
- montecarlo.py gbm subtracts sigma^2/2 each day; the GARCH model subtracts each day's variance / 2 for the same reason.
- options.py: Black-Scholes comes from Ito's lemma applied to a hedged portfolio.

## Watch out
- Volatility drag: a stock that goes +50% then -50% is down 25%. High-volatility assets need a higher average return just to stand still.
